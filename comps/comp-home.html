2.1 resolved, and how. I measured the asset before designing to it. shahedin-cutout.webp carries a genuine subject matte — 33.6% of pixels fully transparent, 7.2% partial (anti-aliased hair edge), and the opaque span per row traces a human silhouette: 166px at the crown, flaring to 795px at the crossed arms. So a free-standing figure is real here, not faked with a clip-path.
Two constraints the asset imposes, designed around rather than hidden. (1) The crown is clipped at source row 0, so a fully-visible figure shows a flat-topped head — the figure is therefore bled off the top of the band with the cut landing on the nav hairline, which reads as him standing behind the nav. (2) The arms touch both frame edges at row ~768, so the figure is cropped to a bust (rows 0-620) where clear transparent margin still exists on both sides, and dissolves below that. The clean fix for both is one asset with headroom and side margin — waist-up, 4:5, ≥1600px long edge. Until then this composition holds.
Decision for you: the hero currently carries three CTAs and four audience figures. Both violate hero-stack discipline, so the comp keeps one primary + one ghost and moves every number into section 2. That demotes প্রোডাক্টস কিনুন out of the hero — it needs a home further down the page. Say the word if the store must stay above the fold.
