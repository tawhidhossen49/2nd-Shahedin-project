1 · Design Read

Reading this as: a redesign-preserve of a Bangla-first creator-education site for YouTube-referred learners and exam candidates, with a warm-ink editorial language, leaning toward native CSS on the existing token system, a Bengali serif display, and a portrait that stands free of every box on the page.

Preserve, not overhaul: the token system, the information architecture, the copy voice and the accent family are already right and stay untouched. What changes is composition — how the page is ruled, where the weight sits, and how many boxes there are.

3 · The concept spine

The record, and the man standing in front of it. The whole site is built as a filed archive — hairlines instead of card borders, indexed numerals down the left of every list, tabular figures, dated captions, years as anchors. Every section is something that has been catalogued: a timeline is an index, a curriculum is a numbered register, press is a row of dated entries, the growth story is five stamped steps. Against all of that ruled, filed, orderly material there is exactly one element that refuses to be contained, and it is Shahedin himself: in the hero he stands free of any frame, the arch behind him is deliberately too small to hold him, and the one hairline that runs the full width of the band is visibly interrupted by his shoulder. That is the argument the page makes before a word is read — the archive is the credential, the person is the reason to stay. It also does the practical work: the ruled system gives eight otherwise-similar sections a shared grammar, and the single break in it tells the eye exactly where to land first.

5 · Section 2.1 — resolved

The de-boxed, free-standing portrait is in comp 1 of 8. I measured the asset before designing to it rather than assuming the filename was accurate, and the measurement is what made the composition possible:

  • shahedin-cutout.webp carries a genuine subject matte, not an edge feather — 33.6% of pixels fully transparent, 59.2% opaque, 7.2% partial (the anti-aliased hair edge).
  • The opaque span per row traces a human silhouette: 166px at the crown, flaring through 400px at the shoulder line (row 465), to 795px at the crossed arms (row 772).

So the figure genuinely stands free — no border, no radius, no overflow:hidden, no background of its own, a real drop-shadow() that follows the alpha, and an elliptical contact shadow grounding it. The arch behind is sized so it cannot contain him: his head clears its top edge and both shoulders clear its sides.

Two constraints fall out of the same measurement, and the composition is built around them rather than hiding them. The crown is clipped at source row 0, so the figure is deliberately bled off the top of the band with the cut landing on the nav hairline — it reads as him standing behind the nav. The arms touch both frame edges at row 768, so the figure is cropped to a bust (rows 0–620, where transparent margin still exists on both sides) and dissolves below that. Both are fixed cleanly by one new asset: waist-up, plain backdrop, headroom above the crown and margin beside the arms, 4:5, ≥1600px long edge. The composition holds without it; it gets better with it.
