Reading this as: a brand-preserve / composition-overhaul redesign of a Bangla-first creator-education site for YouTube-referred learners and exam candidates, with a warm-ink editorial language, leaning toward native CSS on the existing token system, a Bengali serif display, and a portrait that stands free of every box on the page.

The token system, information architecture, copy voice, accent family and JS contract are frozen and stay untouched. What changes is composition: how the page is ruled, where the weight sits, how many boxes there are, and whether motion responds after the first scroll.

3 · The concept spine

The record, and the man standing in front of it. The whole site is built as a filed archive — hairlines instead of card borders, indexed numerals down the left of every list, tabular figures, dated captions, years as anchors. Every section is something that has been catalogued: the timeline is an index, the curriculum is a numbered register, press is a row of dated entries, the growth story is five stamped steps. Against all of that ruled, orderly material there is exactly one element that refuses to be contained, and it is Shahedin himself: in the hero he stands free of any frame, the arc behind him is deliberately too small to hold him, and the one hairline that runs the full width of the band is visibly interrupted by his shoulder. That is the argument the page makes before a word is read — the archive is the credential, the person is the reason to stay. It also does the practical work: the ruled system gives eight otherwise-similar sections a shared grammar, and the single break in it tells the eye exactly where to land first.

5 · Section 2.1 — resolved

In comp 1 of 8. I measured the asset before designing to it rather than trusting the filename, and the measurement is what made the composition possible and what fixed my first attempt.

  • shahedin-cutout.webp carries a genuine subject matte — 33.6% of pixels fully transparent, 59.2% opaque, 7.2% partial (the anti-aliased hair edge).
  • The opaque span per row traces a human silhouette: 166px at the crown, 400px at the shoulder line (row 465), 795px at the crossed arms (row 772).
  • Crop rows 0-800 puts the shoulder line at 58% of the figure. The arc's top edge sits at 63%. Head and both shoulders clear it with 5% to spare — which is the specific thing 2.1 asks for.

No card, no border, no rounded container, no overflow:hidden on the figure. One shape behind it, not a stack. Warm glow spill from the arc onto the silhouette via a drop-shadow that follows the alpha. Base feathered into the band. Grain rebuilt so it actually reads.

Two constraints remain, worked around rather than hidden. The crown is clipped at source row 0, so the figure is bled off the top with the cut landing on the nav hairline. The arms touch both frame edges by row 768, so the crop stops at 800 and feathers out. One photograph with headroom above the crown and margin beside the arms removes both workarounds. The composition holds without it.

6 · What I got wrong in rev.1, and fixed

My first pass predated sight of Part 1 and proposed seven content changes that the frozen contract does not permit. Part 1 wins; all seven are reverted. Listing them because you should know what I tried to do.

Dropped 21 contract-bound data-field attributes. Cut hero.cta3, moved four stats.* out of the hero, cut four more from the trust bar, removed about.image_url, and deleted five *.eyebrow fields. All 21 are restored on the same elements with the same values, verified mechanically.
Removed the story portrait. 2.1 explicitly asks for it to be de-boxed with a different treatment. It is back, using an outlined ring, a tighter head-and-shoulders crop and a cooler flatter grade, so it reads as a second photograph rather than the hero component reused.
Got the 2.1 geometry backwards. My arc's top edge was above the shoulder line, so only the head broke past it — and I stacked two extra background gradients behind the figure, which is the "stack" 2.1 rules out. Both corrected.
Missed three items outright: film grain over the composition, warm glow spill onto the figure's edge, and the oversized display moment. All three are in rev.2.

7 · Where the brief conflicts with itself

One place, and it resolves cleanly, so I have resolved it rather than asking.

Part 1 freezes every Bangla string and every data-field, which includes about.eyebrow, growth.eyebrow, testimonials.eyebrow, press.eyebrow and hero.eyebrow. Part 3 bans "the same eyebrow + h2 pattern opening all nine sections". Deleting eyebrows satisfies Part 3 and breaks Part 1.

Resolution: keep all five strings and all five data-field attributes in the DOM, and vary the treatment so the repeated pattern disappears. Hero keeps the pulse pill; story absorbs its eyebrow into the h2 as a lead-in line; growth sets it tiny directly above 9rem display type; testimonials baseline-aligns it to the right of the headline; press prefixes it with an accent hairline. Same strings, same contract, five different patterns, and three sections carry no eyebrow at all.

8 · Tier 2 motion — the five for the homepage

2.3 caps the homepage at five. Chosen, with the reason each earns its place:

  1. Scrub-linked scroll on the hero portrait and its arc. Serves 2.1 directly — the figure and the shape behind it separate as you scroll, which is what proves he is free-standing rather than a placed image.
  2. Word-level stagger on the oversized growth headline. The one display moment on the page earns the one type animation.
  3. Clip-path wipe on section headings. Reinforces the ruled-archive spine: headings are revealed by a moving rule, not faded in.
  4. Magnetic hero primary CTA. One element, small radius. The single most important click on the site.
  5. Card lift with tinted shadow and 1-2° tilt on course rows and video facades. Response on the two things people actually point at.

Not chosen: the pinned section, because no section on this page has content that changes while it holds; and the marquee, because a scrolling strip would fight the ruled-archive spine that the whole design rests on. Other pages get two each.
